Research blueprint

Turn a broad topic into bounded, verifiable research.

Use it when
you need to understand a topic before deciding or producing.
Example
Generative AI for a non-technical sales team.
Output evidence
questions, scope, priority sources, tensions and sufficiency rule.
1Level 1
I need to understand [TOPIC] for [DECISION OR OUTCOME]. My context is [CONTEXT], audience [AUDIENCE], and horizon [DATE OR REGION]. Bound the scope; frame essential questions; separate facts, interpretations and uncertainty; prioritize primary sources with author, date, link and reason; identify tensions and gaps. End with an observable rule for deciding when the base is sufficient. Do not invent sources or certainty.

Learn

Source-grounded coach

Learn through questions, explanations and citations from a defined source base.

Use it when
you have sources and want understanding rather than a summary.
Example
Explain RAG to an operations lead through analogies and counterexamples.
Output evidence
cited explanation, retrieval question and next source to review.
1Level 1
Act as a learning coach on [TOPIC]. Use only [SOURCES OR NOTEBOOK] and cite every material claim. First ask what I want to achieve and what I think I understand. Then explain one idea at a time, ask me to restate it, diagnose errors and use a counterexample. Separate evidence, inference and missing data. End each round with one retrieval question and the exact source I should review.

Learn

Deep research

Close the highest-impact gap without uncontrolled scope growth.

Use it when
an audit reveals a gap blocking your outcome.
Example
Check legal and operational limits of an internal copilot.
Output evidence
cited report addressing the gap and stating residual risk.
1Level 1
Research the gap [GAP] because it affects [OUTCOME]. Include the main question; in- and out-of-scope questions; time and region; preferred primary sources; minimum evidence; competing positions; and acceptance criteria. Deliver cited findings, a claim | evidence | confidence | limit table, contradictions and residual risk. If material context is missing, ask no more than three questions first.

Cross-check

Verify a claim before using it as a foundation.

Use it when
an answer sounds convincing but its evidence is uncertain.
Example
Verify whether an AI feature exists in the contracted plan.
Output evidence
confirmed, refuted or not-demonstrable verdict with traceability.
1Level 1
Verify [CLAIM] for [USE]. Seek independent evidence and prioritize the original source. For each source record author, date, link, relevant excerpt or data and limitation. Distinguish genuine corroboration from source repetition. Issue CONFIRMED, REFUTED or NOT DEMONSTRABLE. Explain what would change the verdict and what must not yet be claimed.

Relevance audit

Decide what to retain, update or remove from a learning base.

Use it when
your library has grown and may no longer serve its purpose.
Example
Review 20 Notebook sources before preparing a class.
Output evidence
retain/update/remove matrix and sufficiency verdict.
1Level 1
Audit [SOURCE BASE] against [PURPOSE]. Assess authority, freshness, coverage, redundancy, bias, contradictions and usage rights. Deliver a short summary; source | decision | evidence | impact | action table; prioritized gaps; and BASE SUFFICIENT or REPEAT RESEARCH verdict. Do not reward volume: keep only what supports understanding or decision.

Practice generator

Turn knowledge into retrieval, application and correction tasks.

Use it when
an explanation feels clear but use has not been demonstrated.
Example
Practise selecting AI use cases through realistic scenarios.

1Level 1
Design a practice on [TOPIC] for [AUDIENCE] using [SOURCES]. Include four moments: unaided retrieval, explanation in my own words, application to a new case and evidence-based correction. you must present what you learned without losing nuance or traceability.

Propose a concise visual structure and speaker notes. Then rehearse one section at a time, challenge weak claims and finish with likely questions and cited answers.
