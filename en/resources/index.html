A complete path for getting started

Choose the resource that answers your next question.

Opening everything at once adds friction and hides what you need to learn first.

Four resources serve different jobs: understanding, practising, repeating the method and writing instructions.

Use the masterclass for context, workbook for practice, playbook for continuity and prompts for direction.

You chose well if the resource produces an observable decision or result.
