Designed for teams

Align the team across 18 moments

Turn this resource into an operational capability the team can govern.

The operating challenge

The challenge is not deploying more AI, but reducing variability, risk and untraceable work.

How to implement it

Work with accountable owners, authorized sources, controls, metrics and explicit human review.

What to document

Record source, decision, risk, owner and outcome; do not promote pilots without evidence.

Gain a shared pattern, control criteria and evidence for prioritizing investment and adoption.

Choose a bounded workflow and define its owner, control, metric and exit criterion.Value appears when the practice is repeatable, auditable and sustainable for the team.
