Why it worksAcceptance criteria

Acceptance criteria

  • Every block has a source or a marker.
  • The summary separates facts from inference.
  • At least one relevant gap is identified.
  • The diagnosis has a documented custodian, reviewer and permissions.
  • The recommendation retains a date, outcome and reviewable evidence.
  • The accountable owner, reviewer and shared evidence are identified.

Edge cases

  • Incomplete records: deliver a partial map.
  • Overlapping activities: keep both and mark conflict.
  • Incomplete week: return a partial reading and mark the missing period.

Trade-offs

  • More detail improves traceability but increases recording effort. Governance adds coordination but reduces opaque decisions.

Assumptions

  • The declared inputs, permissions and sources represent the case under review.

Limits

  • Describe the observed period; do not predict performance.
  • It does not authorize access, change or automatic adoption.

Edge cases
