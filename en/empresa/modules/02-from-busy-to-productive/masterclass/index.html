From busy to productive

Turn a real week into a visible priority system: three justified outcomes, protected blocks, and a final review. This guide complements the official PDF without replacing it.

For teams

Make the work that deserves attention visible.

A full calendar does not prove progress. Without explicit criteria, urgent work displaces important work. Missing agreements also scatter team attention.

What you will gain

Leave with defensible priorities, protected time, and a simple way to review progress.

How to proceed

Diagnose, decide, block, execute, and review. Each step leaves an observable artifact.

What to verify

Deliver a short activity log, three justified priorities, a protected calendar, and a review with decisions for the next week.

Build my system

Repeat the system weekly and change one rule when evidence reveals recurring friction.

From activity to progress

Activity describes movement; progress requires a useful completed result. The team agrees the result and who will check the output.

+3 min Explore this moment further before moving on.

Key points

  • Name the result before listing tasks.
  • Use observable evidence to recognize progress.
Application
Take a recent task and rewrite it as a verifiable result.
Check your understanding
What fact would prove that you moved forward? State who will review the evidence.
Short practice
Rewrite one activity as result, action and evidence.

Moment 03 · 7 min03 / 11

Value before volume

Task volume does not show value; a result must serve a stated purpose. Affected areas review how their work is classified.

+3 min Explore this moment further before moving on.
Go deeperDiagnose

Key points

  • Separate value creation, maintenance, coordination and noise.
  • Do not erase necessary maintenance: expose its cost.
Application
Classify five recent activities and justify one uncertain category.
Check your understanding
Which activity consumes time without supporting a necessary result? Confirm which area must validate the category.
Short practice
Place one ambiguous activity on the map and note the missing evidence.
Keep this idea
Value is argued, not assumed.

Three priorities

Protected calendar

A priority without reserved time loses to immediate work. Each block coordinates accountability, inputs and output review.

Go deeperDesign
