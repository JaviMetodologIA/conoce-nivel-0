Glossary

Glossary

Friction map
A record of waiting, rework, handoffs, and repeated decisions.
Mini-SOP
A concise, versioned procedure with steps, criteria, and exceptions.
Human gate
A point before an effect where a person decides.
AAR
A review of expectation, result, evidence, and adjustment.
PASA
Plan, accelerate, systematize, and amplify what is stable.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does this authorize autonomy?

No. It covers co-creation with roles and human gates.

Who approves?

The owner with review required by policy and risk.

When adopt?

After a bounded, reversible pilot and transfer.

What if data changes?

Invalidate the affected part and repeat approval and testing.

Next step

Checklist
  • Owner, reviewer, and escalation are clear.
  • Controls and traceability remain.
  • Pilot and AAR support changes.
  • Adoption is reversible.
