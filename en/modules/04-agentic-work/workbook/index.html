Module 04 · Workbook

Workbook: first agentic pilot

Move from a candidate task to a supervised mission with limits, evidence and a continuation decision.

Journey outcomeKeep the brief, approved plan, exceptions, sources used, result and final decision.
StartMasterclass
From doing to orchestrating

Delegate a process without giving up your judgment.

Giving an AI a vague goal can accelerate the wrong outcome. Useful autonomy begins by stating what it may decide, what it must escalate and how success is checked.

What you will produce

You finish with a mission brief, permission matrix, approved plan and the record of one small, reversible run.

How to proceed

Choose a case, design the contract, limit tools, approve the plan, observe execution and decide whether to keep, adjust or stop.

What to verify

Keep the brief, approved plan, exceptions, sources used, result and final decision.

Design my pilot

Expand autonomy only after the pilot passes; otherwise adjust or stop.

00 · How to use this workbook

How to use this workbook

Start in class, deepen through practice, and consolidate only with reviewable evidence.

  1. 01

    Frame the caseState the case, desired result and evidence you want to observe.

  2. 02

    Prepare the inputsGather what is needed, acknowledge boundaries and use the three preparation prompts.

  3. 03

    Work in classComplete the essential practice and retain initial evidence.

  4. 04

    Go deeperOpen each detail to review actions, criteria, examples and connections.

  5. 05

    Consolidate and transferMeet the criterion, review the evidence and test the practice in another context.

Orientation

Personal pilot, visible control

Complete the workbook with an individual mission that has no external effect. Every step leaves a decision and evidence you can review. In this pilot, any execution happens outside the workbook and requires separate authorization.

Use case
Organize private notes into a weekly draft without sending or publishing it.
You are ready when
You have a repeatable case, its sources and time to review every output.

Inputs

  • Three repeatable personal tasks
  • Authorized private sources
  • A testable result
  • A stop condition

How to apply it

Prompts · prompt-08
Transfer

Expand autonomy only after the pilot passes; otherwise adjust or stop.

Design a bounded first agentic workflow: clear objective, minimum permissions, plan approval and evidence before expanding autonomy.

Consolidation criterion

Transferable

Contract, execution, exceptions and verdict can be reviewed and adapted without widening authority.

Expected evidence
Versioned brief, approved plan, case log and verdict.
Review
Review after one complete case; do not widen scope by calendar.

Transfer challenge: one mission, one decision

Apply the package to a new case and hold scope until its own evidence supports a decision.

  • State what stays and what changes.
  • Revalidate sources, permissions and plan.
  • Close with keep, adjust or stop.
Evidence
Versioned brief, approved plan, case log and verdict.
Review
Review after one complete case; do not widen scope by calendar.
