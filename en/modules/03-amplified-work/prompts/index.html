Why it worksAcceptance criteria

Acceptance criteria

  • Has scope and owner.
  • A case exists.
  • Boundary is written.
  • The Mini-SOP retains a version, owner, exception and test evidence.
  • You can explain what you decided, the evidence used and what you will review next.

Edge cases

  • If no case exists, state that evidence is absent.
  • Workflow without a boundary: define start and finish before improving it.
  • Conflicting practices: record which one will be tested and why.

Trade-offs

  • Less scope gives more control.

Assumptions

  • The declared inputs, permissions and sources represent the case under review.

Limits

  • Does not execute.
