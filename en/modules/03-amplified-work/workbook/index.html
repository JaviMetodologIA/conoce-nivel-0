Module 03 · Workbook

Workbook: design and test your workflow

Choose a task, observe your practice, compare sources, co-create the Mini-SOP, and test one case under human review.

Journey outcomeA Mini-SOP with input, steps, criteria, human review, output, exceptions, and a real-case test log.
StartMasterclass
For your practice

Turn one real task into a way of working you can repeat.

When every delivery depends on memory and improvisation, you repeat decisions, carry friction, and cannot tell what AI improved.

What you will gain

You will leave with a clear personal workflow, a test log, and a version you can reuse without losing control.

How to proceed

Choose a task, observe your practice, compare sources, co-create the Mini-SOP, and test one case under human review.

What to verify

A Mini-SOP with input, steps, criteria, human review, output, exceptions, and a real-case test log.

Design my workflow

Repeat the workflow on a second case and change only what the log supports.

00 · How to use this workbook

How to use this workbook

Start in class, deepen through practice, and consolidate only with reviewable evidence.

  1. 01

    Frame the caseState the case, desired result and evidence you want to observe.

  2. 02

    Prepare the inputsGather what is needed, acknowledge boundaries and use the three preparation prompts.

  3. 03

    Work in classComplete the essential practice and retain initial evidence.

  4. 04

    Go deeperOpen each detail to review actions, criteria, examples and connections.

  5. 05

    Consolidate and transferMeet the criterion, review the evidence and test the practice in another context.
