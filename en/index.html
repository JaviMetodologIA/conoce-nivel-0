AI: what is happening and how to benefit

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. The route starts from basic conversational use and emphasizes method and judgment.

Is the cohort free?

Yes. The current public form states free workshops and up to 25 people per cycle.

Must I take all four classes?

You can start with the first one. The complete route is designed to accumulate capabilities.

What happens to my data?

The landing collects no answers. Registration opens a public Google Form.

If you made it this far, I want to leave you with one simple idea: AI does not have to pull you away from your way of thinking. Used well, it can help you see it more clearly, test it and turn it into action.
