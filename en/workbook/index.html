Turn one of your questions into learning you can repeat.

The workbook orders preparation, practice and consolidation so you do not depend on one conversation.

Define the topic, load relevant sources, run one instruction and record how your understanding changed.

Keep citations, decisions, open doubts and a result you can rebuild without copying the answer.

Why it works

Acceptance criteria

  • Every technical term of the dictation appears unchanged in the clean version, with no paraphrase
  • The confirmation holds five points or fewer and each one can be marked right or corrected
  • Questions arrive one at a time: the next appears only after the previous answer

Edge cases

  • Unintelligible dictation: a new recording is requested instead of filling the gap
  • Missing session purpose: it is requested before cleaning, because the ordering depends on it

Trade-offs

  • Fidelity to the voice in exchange for less polished prose
  • This turn does not separate authorship because it assumes a single voice; in exchange it exposes the cleaning-level control that the team cell pins to conservative
  • The original definition of done promised that the person "discovers possibilities": it is replaced by facts of the turn — points confirmed, terms preserved, questions answered — because discovery is neither produced nor verified by the prompt

Assumptions

  • The dictation is the only available material: there are no implicit attachments

Limits

  • It confirms understanding; it neither classifies the intent nor writes the study plan
  • It proposes no material and designs no Notebook, and it does not promise the understanding is right: it only makes it reviewable

Why it works

Acceptance criteria

  • The study plan carries the nine sections in the requested order and no section is left empty without saying why
  • The minimum evidence can be called done or not done by someone who does not know the intent
  • Every section comes back marked as confirmed or corrected, not approved in bulk

Edge cases

  • Mixed intent: the blocking one is declared main, with the secondary one placed after it
  • Constraint or deadline never dictated: it is requested instead of completed

Trade-offs

  • One more step before touching tools in exchange for not building the wrong Notebook
  • This turn asks for evidence tailored to me and does not require comparability across people, which is what the team cell adds along with the deciding role
  • The original definition of done promised the study plan "enables a later decision about the needed Notebook and sources": that happens in a later turn, so what is checked here is what this turn does produce — confirmed sections and declarable evidence

Assumptions

  • The understanding arrives already confirmed; if not, the prompt itself runs a minimal confirmation before classifying
  • The final decision is human in every case

Why it works

Acceptance criteria

  • Every candidate item appears in exactly one of the three lists, and every excluded one carries its reason
  • The recommended role comes with a boundary sentence saying what it will not do
  • The five workshop steps are named, not cited as an external reference

Edge cases

  • Empty study plan: it is confirmed in five points before planning instead of being inferred
  • Zero loadable material: the plan stops with coverage_gap declared

Trade-offs

  • A conservative plan in exchange for a clean start with nothing that has to be pulled back later
  • This turn excludes material by privacy or rights case by case on my own judgement; the team cell treats exclusion as policy and adds an external reviewer
  • The original definition of done said the plan "lets the person open NotebookLM and begin": that happens outside the turn, so what is checked here are the pieces the plan does deliver

Assumptions

  • The study plan arrives confirmed from an earlier turn; if not, the prompt itself confirms it before planning
  • The candidate material is what the person names: there are no implicit attachments

Limits

  • It promises no tool capability beyond what is documented, and no accuracy of the answers

Why it works

Acceptance criteria

  • The report carries the four fixed sections: scope, essentials, tensions and recommended sources
  • Every recommended source carries title, author, date, link and reason; without the five fields it stays out
  • Every uncertain line carries the tag "uncertain: X is missing"; untagged lines read as asserted

Edge cases

  • No reachable primary sources: the report ships shorter and the gaps stay listed for the research plan
  • No decision or result declared: the prompt asks before scoping

Trade-offs

  • An imperfect first report in exchange for opening the cycle with material of my own that can be audited
  • A fixed cutoff leaves later publications out, in exchange for making "recent changes" checkable

Assumptions

  • The report will be imported into the Notebook as the first source and audited in the next step
  • The anatomy of scoping, splitting facts from interpretations and prioritizing primary sources with a reason is inherited from the standalone variant 01; here it ends in a cited report, not in a sufficiency rule
  • If prior preparation happened, the preplan orients the scope; it brings no loaded sources: the Notebook is still empty when step 1 starts

Limits

  • It produces the first base, not a sufficient base: sufficiency is decided when the cycle closes
  • It does not audit or rank the gaps of what it delivers: that belongs to W02

Why it works

Acceptance criteria

  • Exactly 10 starter prompts, none repeating what another covers
  • The test guide includes an out-of-base question that passes only if the assistant declares the limit
  • The instruction declares limits and a citation rule coherent with the sufficiency verdict already issued, not a new one

Edge cases

  • Constraints clashing with the purpose: it stops before writing the instruction
  • Private material: the non-exposure limit is written

Trade-offs

  • A conservative configuration in exchange for less apparent reach: the assistant promises what the base supports
  • The workshop metadata is preserved whole: the when becomes the when field, the source-grounded coach becomes example, the configuration with 10 starters becomes evidence, the do-not-invent-capabilities limit becomes a guardrail and the purpose, user and limits inputs become the variables; the non-exposure limit for private material lives in guardrails and edge cases because the prompt budget cannot hold all three conditions at once

Assumptions

  • The base arrives audited and declared sufficient in earlier steps; the audit configuration is input, not the final instruction

Limits

  • It configures the assistant; it neither re-audits sources nor issues a sufficiency verdict: that closed in earlier workshop steps; from 07 the recommended configuration arrives, not the final instruction
  • It does not rank which cases are worth covering: that is W07
  • It authorizes neither publication nor connectors: RENDERED_DRAFT is not published

Useful assistants

In 2026 assistants coexist with more autonomous agents. They remain useful for a clear function, stable instructions, fast creation, and controllable token use. They do not replace judgment or turn weak sources into evidence.

Reusable skills

A skill is a reusable package of instructions, criteria, resources, and checks that teaches an AI how to execute a task consistently. It must declare sources, limits, inputs, outputs, and tests.
